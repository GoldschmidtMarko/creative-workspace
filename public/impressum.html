Website purpose

BAX Checker is a free, non-commercial tool that analyses German badminton tournament entries — showing each player's BAX rating, league history and activity, and comparing team strength — using publicly available data from dbv.turnier.de and badminton-bax.de.

No commercial activities, advertising, or monetization takes place on this website.
